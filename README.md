# My Favorite Series

## Wednesday

![Wednesday](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/0/06/Wednesday_Netflix_series_poster.png)

### About the Series

Wednesday is a mystery and comedy series about a girl named Wednesday Addams. She studies at Nevermore Academy, where she meets different students and discovers mysterious events.

### Main Character

- **Name:** Wednesday Addams
- **Genre:** Mystery, Comedy, Fantasy
- **Platform:** Netflix

### Why I Like This Series

I like Wednesday because it has an interesting story and unexpected events. I also like the main character because she is smart, brave, and different from other people.

### My Favorite Part

My favorite part is when Wednesday solves mysteries and discovers the truth about the strange events happening around her.
